# 💬 Interactive Prompt Guides

The **PARTS** framework in the Google AI Educator training offers a structured, chronological approach to enhancing interactions with Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents. This method is particularly beneficial for both educators and learners.

**See also:** [Interactive Prompt Examples](https://github.com/devservice-ora/AI/blob/main/Common%20AI%20prompt%20examples.md)

---

## 📖 PARTS and Overview of each component

1. **P**urpose: Define your objective for the interaction to guide the conversation effectively.
2. **A**udience: Tailor prompts to your audience's knowledge and interests for more engaging responses.
3. **R**elevance: Include specific, context-rich details in your prompts to improve AI's understanding and output quality.
4. **T**one: Specify the desired tone (formal or informal) to align the AI's responses with your expectations.
5. **S**tructure: Organize your prompts logically for clearer and more digestible answers.

## 📖 Related definition

1. **P**ersona: Identify who you are.
2. **A**im: Clearly state your objective—what do you wish to achieve?
3. **R**ecipients: Specify your audience.
4. **T**heme: The tone should be conversational and engaging.
5. **S**tructure: Format in a clear and organized structure.

By following the **PARTS** framework and the sequence of steps, users can optimize AI-interactive prompts, leading to more effective results and a better understanding of AI technologies.

---
📌 Google’s AI Principles require a **human-in-the-loop** approach: The AI provides the first draft, but you leverage your expertise to make the final decision.
