# 💬 Interactive Prompt Guides

In my Google AI Educator training, the **PARTS** framework is a structured, chronological order in which it comes to mind for enhancing interactions with Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents, especially beneficial for educators and learners. 

## 📖 PARTS and Overview of each component

1. **P**urpose: Define your objective for the interaction to guide the conversation effectively.
2. **A**udience: Tailor prompts based on the knowledge and interests of your audience for more engaging responses.
3. **R**elevance: Include specific and contextual details in your prompts to improve AI understanding and output quality.
4. **T**one: Specify the desired tone (formal or informal) to align the AI's responses with your expectations.
5. **S**tructure: Organize your prompts logically for clearer and more digestible answers.

## 📖 Related definition

1. **P**ersona: Identify who you are.
2. **A**im: State your objective clearly—what exactly do you want to achieve?
3. **R**ecipients: Specify your audience.
4. **T**heme: The tone should be conversational and engaging.
5. **S**tructure: Format in a clear and organized structure.

By following the **PARTS** framework and the sequence of steps, users can optimize AI-interactive prompts, leading to more effective results and a better understanding of AI technologies.
